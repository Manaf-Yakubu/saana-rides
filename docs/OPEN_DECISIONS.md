# Open decisions

These are business rules the design document left undecided. Each one is implemented as an audited, configurable setting with the default below. The table lists the defaults currently in force; management can change them.

| #   | Topic                                 | Default in force                                                                                                                                                                       | Setting key (planned)                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Vehicle types                         | Two configurable types, KEKE and CAMBUU, both assumed three-wheeled passenger vehicles                                                                                                 | `fleet.vehicle_types`                           |
| 2   | Contract total                        | Computed as installment x payable periods (+ deposit). An optional explicit management override is stored alongside the computed value                                                 | `contracts.allow_total_override`                |
| 3   | Payable days (DAILY)                  | All 7 days payable. Rest days and holidays are configurable                                                                                                                            | `schedule.daily.rest_days`, `schedule.holidays` |
| 4   | Monthly due dates                     | Clamped to the last day of a shorter month (Accra calendar dates)                                                                                                                      | (rule)                                          |
| 5   | Deposit                               | Part of the total, due on the activation date as installment 0                                                                                                                         | `packages.deposit_amount`                       |
| 6   | Late fees                             | Off by default. When on, they are tracked as separate `contract_charges`, not added to the total. They must be cleared before an ownership transfer                                    | `payments.late_fee.*`                           |
| 7   | Grace period                          | 2 days, then the installment becomes OVERDUE                                                                                                                                           | `payments.grace_period_days`                    |
| 8   | Prepayment                            | Allowed. Surplus is allocated oldest-first to future installments; no floating credit. Overpaying beyond the remaining balance is rejected                                             | `payments.allow_prepayment`                     |
| 9   | Partial payments                      | Allowed. Minimum amount 0                                                                                                                                                              | `payments.min_amount`                           |
| 10  | Overdue recovery                      | The contract returns to ACTIVE automatically when nothing is overdue                                                                                                                   | (rule)                                          |
| 11  | Manual (non-cash) payments in Phase 1 | MoMo and bank payments recorded by staff stay PENDING until Finance confirms, the same as cash                                                                                         | `payments.require_confirmation_methods`         |
| 12  | Vehicle ASSIGNED vs ACTIVE            | ASSIGNED is set when the contract activates; ACTIVE is set when management marks the vehicle handed over. **Needs business confirmation**                                              | (rule)                                          |
| 13  | Termination / reassignment            | The old contract becomes TERMINATED with a balance snapshot. Nothing carries over to a new contract automatically. What happens to money already paid is recorded as a management note | `contracts.termination.*`                       |
| 14  | Cancellation                          | Management only, with a reason. The vehicle returns to AVAILABLE. No automatic refunds                                                                                                 | `contracts.cancellation.*`                      |
| 15  | Staff types                           | One staff member may hold several staff types                                                                                                                                          | (rule)                                          |
| 16  | First admin                           | Created with a CLI command; must enrol in TOTP on first login                                                                                                                          | (rule)                                          |
| 17  | Live contracts per driver             | At most one live contract per driver, the same as per vehicle                                                                                                                          | `contracts.max_live_per_driver`                 |
| 18  | Receipts in Phase 1                   | PDF receipt plus a signed link. SMS uses a log-only adapter until Phase 2                                                                                                              | `notifications.sms.provider`                    |
| 19  | Daily-sales notifications             | The driver is notified only when a daily-sales entry is VERIFIED or DISPUTED                                                                                                           | (rule)                                          |
| 20  | Ownership eligibility                 | Fully paid, schedule fully PAID, no unpaid charges, no open disputes or issues, paperwork done. Extra checklist items are configurable                                                 | `transfer.checklist_items`                      |
| 21  | Transfer approver                     | Management role only                                                                                                                                                                   | `transfer.approver_roles`                       |
| 22  | Data-subject deletion (Act 843)       | PII is anonymised; financial and contract records are retained for 7 years. **Needs legal confirmation**                                                                               | `privacy.retention_years`                       |
| 23  | Ride cancellation fee (Phase 3)       | Free within 2 minutes of acceptance, then a flat fee. A driver no-show is free for the passenger                                                                                       | `rides.cancellation.*`                          |
